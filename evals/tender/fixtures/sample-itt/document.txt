INSTRUCTIONS TO TENDERERS

Project: Construction of a 24,000 m2 pre-engineered steel warehouse and
ancillary office building at Phnom Penh Special Economic Zone.
Employer: Meakara Industrial Holdings Co., Ltd.
Consultant: Angkor Design Partnership Ltd.
Tender Reference: MIH-PPSEZ-2026-014

SECTION 1 GENERAL

1.1 Scope of these Instructions
These Instructions to Tenderers form part of the Tender Documents and shall
be read in conjunction with the Conditions of Contract, the Employer's
Requirements, the Technical Specifications, the Drawings and the Bill of
Quantities.

[[page 2]]
2. TENDER DOCUMENTS

2.1 Composition
The Tender Documents comprise: Instructions to Tenderers; Form of Tender and
Appendix to Tender; Conditions of Contract; Employer's Requirements;
Technical Specifications; Bill of Quantities; Drawings; and any Addenda.

2.2 Examination
Tenderers are expected to examine all instructions, forms, terms and
specifications. Failure to furnish all information required will be at the
Tenderer's risk and may result in rejection of the tender.

2.3 Addenda
At any time prior to the deadline for submission, the Employer may amend the
Tender Documents by issuing an Addendum. Each Addendum shall be numbered
sequentially and shall form part of the Tender Documents. The Tenderer shall
acknowledge receipt of each Addendum in writing, and shall confirm in the
Form of Tender that the tender price includes the effect of all Addenda.

[[page 4]]
3. TENDER VALIDITY

3.1 Period
Tenders shall remain valid and open for acceptance for a period of one
hundred and twenty (120) calendar days from the deadline for submission of
tenders. A tender valid for a shorter period shall be rejected as
non-responsive.

3.2 Extension
In exceptional circumstances, prior to expiry of the tender validity period,
the Employer may request that Tenderers extend the period of validity.

[[page 5]]
4. TENDER SECURITY

4.1 Amount
The Tenderer shall furnish, as part of its tender, a Tender Security (bid
bond) in the amount of two per cent (2%) of the tender price.

4.2 Form and issuer
The Tender Security shall be issued by a commercial bank licensed by the
National Bank of Cambodia, shall be in the form provided in Section 8, and
shall be denominated in United States Dollars.

4.3 Validity
The Tender Security shall remain valid for a period of twenty-eight (28)
days beyond the expiry of the tender validity period. Any tender not
accompanied by a substantially responsive Tender Security shall be rejected.

4.3.1 Return of security
The Tender Security of unsuccessful Tenderers will be returned within
twenty-eight (28) days of the expiration of the tender validity or the
signing of the Contract, whichever is earlier.

[[page 7]]
5. QUALIFICATION OF THE TENDERER

5.1 Legal status
The Tenderer shall submit a valid Certificate of Incorporation issued by the
Ministry of Commerce, a valid Patent Tax Certificate, and a valid VAT
registration certificate.

5.2 Financial capacity
The Tenderer shall demonstrate an average annual construction turnover of
not less than USD 8,000,000 over the last three (3) financial years,
supported by audited financial statements.

5.3 Experience
The Tenderer shall demonstrate successful completion, as main contractor, of
at least two (2) projects of a similar nature within the last seven (7)
years, each with a contract value of not less than USD 4,000,000.

5.4 Liquidity
The Tenderer shall demonstrate access to liquid assets or credit facilities
of not less than USD 1,500,000, supported by a bank reference letter issued
within the last ninety (90) days.

[[page 11]]
7. PRICING AND THE BILL OF QUANTITIES

7.1 Pricing every item
The Tenderer shall price every item in the Bill of Quantities. Any item left
unpriced shall be deemed to have been included within the rates and prices
of other items.

7.2 Currency and fluctuation
Rates and prices shall be quoted in United States Dollars and shall be
deemed to be fixed for the duration of the Contract. No adjustment for
fluctuation in the cost of labour, materials or plant shall be permitted.
Clear internal height shall be not less than 12.5 m throughout.
